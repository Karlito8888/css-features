@page sets the size, the margins and the margin boxes of the printed page, and a selector such as :first or :left limits it to some pages. It acts when printing, and the rules of a preview frame never reach the page you print: add them to your own stylesheet. Size and margins: Chrome, Firefox, Safari 18.2+, except the landscape and portrait keywords, which Safari does not support; margin boxes: Chrome 131+ only.
