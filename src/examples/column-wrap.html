Fog sat on the bay at six.

The tide was low, and the boats lay still on wet sand.

A gull stood guard on a post and watched the shore.

By ten the sun was up and the sea came in fast.

Kids ran out to count each wave as it broke.

At dusk the lamps came on and the quay went quiet.
