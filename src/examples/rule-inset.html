rule-inset sets the cap insets (ends at the edge of the grid), then after a slash the junction insets (ends at a crossing gap), of the column rules (indigo) and the row rules (pink) together. Positive values shorten the rule, negative values lengthen it. overlap-join extends a segment into the crossing gap, up to the other rule (0 at the edge of the grid). Chrome 149+ only.
