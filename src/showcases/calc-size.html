This browser does not support calc-size(), so the panel stays closed.

Open the panel, then drag the factor. The height is the content height times the factor, and it animates.

height: calc-size(auto, size * 1)

Content height

The panel reads the natural height of what is inside it, multiplies it, and lands exactly there.

Below 1 the content is cut. Above 1 the panel keeps extra room. At every step the height stays a real, animatable length.
