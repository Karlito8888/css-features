::details-content is not supported in this browser, so the panels open without animation.

Click a question. Only one panel stays open.

What is animated here?

Only the panel body. The summary stays put while its content grows from zero height.

Why does one panel close the other?

The three panels share the same name attribute, so opening one closes the others.

Do I need JavaScript?

No. The panel opens and closes with the plain details element, and the animation is one CSS transition on its content.
