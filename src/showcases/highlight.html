Custom highlights are not supported in this browser. Searching will not mark the text.

A custom highlight paints a range of text without touching the markup. The text stays a plain text node, so nothing is wrapped and nothing reflows.

Type a word above. Each match becomes a range, and every range joins one named highlight. The browser draws the highlight, and the page styles it with a single rule.

Use the buttons to move between matches. The current match belongs to a second highlight with a higher priority, so its color wins over the others.
