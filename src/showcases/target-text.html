Harbour notes

The morning starts with the slow tide pulling nets across the quay, and the gulls argue over the first fish of the day.

By noon the market fills with voices. Children chase each other between stalls, and an old woman sells oranges from a basket older than the harbour wall.

At dusk the copper lanterns are lit one by one along the pier. Their light spills on the water and the bay turns the colour of tea.

Nobody hurries after dark. The last ferry leaves at ten, and the lamplighter walks home whistling the same song.
